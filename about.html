Joe's Blurbs

About me

Hi, I'm Joe. Joseph Lee Graham on paper, Joe Graham to just about everyone.

I think I was a photographer before I knew it. I grew up in the woods of Alabama and still live there. Some trees grow best in the shade, and mine was a family legacy of history, service, and community. It gave me a deep respect for the past and a quiet, watchful way of looking at the world.

When I finally bought my first camera in college, it felt like we belonged together. I changed my major from history to photography, took a short detour into film, and never looked back. I graduated from UAB with a degree in photography, classically trained: I can mix chemicals and load a changing bag in the dark.

After school I interned with a local commercial photographer, assisted a few high-end wedding photographers, and cut my teeth shooting portraits at a busy storefront studio. Then life shifted, and I set the professional camera down for the best job there is: raising my family.

Over the last few years I've come back to photography deeper than ever, starting in the Alabama woods with nature, wildlife, and the stories tucked into local landscapes. I'm having a blast pushing my craft.

The history major never really left. You'll find it in the journal, under the pictures.

Who I'd like to work with

Now that I'm back behind the lens, I'd like to work with people who care about real, intentional pictures.

Nature and conservation projects
Groups, publications, and local organizations that need patient visual storytelling about regional wildlife and habitat.
Local businesses and artisans
Makers who want real images of their process, products, or spaces, without the stock-photo feel.
Editorial and historical collaborators
Writers, publishers, and preservationists who need a curious eye for the mood and detail of a place or a story.

If your project needs patience and a lot of respect for the subject, let's talk.
