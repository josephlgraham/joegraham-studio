What the records say

Conscription and death4
  • Priscilla, 27 Jul 1872: "My husband John Graham died in the year 1863. He was conscripted into the Confederate army in 1863." She says he was a Union man who "held out for some time and did not go till he was forced to go by the conscript officers."Claim 9849, images 00410 and 00413Documented (her sworn testimony)
  • William, 1872: "I am sure claimant's husband was conscripted and carried to the conscript camps at Talladega, Alabama, for I went with him at the time he was carried off. He died as a conscript in the Confederate army, but I do not recollect the year in which he died."Claim 9849, image 00417Documented (sworn)
  • J. W. Rowe: he saw him "in conscript camps myself in 1863"; "Claimant's husband died in the Confederate army at Mobile, Alabama, as a conscript."Claim 9849, image 00415Documented (sworn)
  • The rehearing petition for William's claim names two deceased sons, John and Thomas: "John Graham did not volunteer. He remained at home until 1863, when he was conscripted and put into that army, and died the same year."Claim 7099, image 00443Documented (the claimant's statement)
Other records3
  • Family book: "John Graham hid out down in the wooded and bluff area, not wanting to be forced to go to war. Once when he was home to see his family, someone notified the Sheriff's office that he was home ... he ran out the back door of the house and as he jumped the fence in the backyard, he was shot to death."Family book p127 (printed, retelling)Family oral history
  • The sworn accounts say he died in the army, at Mobile; the family book says he was shot at home. The two do not agree and both are kept.Claims 9849, 7099 and the family book p127Unresolved
  • The 21st Alabama service record: the description on it (age 46, born Canada, a sailor) does not match this John (19 in 1850, Alabama, farmer), and the cards disagree with each other (one has a John Graham dying 8 Nov 1863 at Fort Morgan, others have one present in Mobile in December 1863).
