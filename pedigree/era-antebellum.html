Graham-name entries in the tract book for T15S R3W in these years. Sections open on the land map.

  1. 19 Jun 1836
    William Graham: NW¼ SE¼ Sec. 28, 40.13 acres, cash, paid $50.17, patent 20 Sep 1839, certificate 19542.Four days before his father. Patented the same day as Thomas's.Tract book entryDocumented
  2. 23 Jun 1836
    Thompson Graham: NW¼ NE¼ Sec. 26, 40.08 acres, cash, paid $50.10, patent 20 Sep 1839, certificate 19602. Written "Thomas Graham".The first Graham entry we can date. He was about sixty.Tract book entryDocumented
  3. 6 Oct 1836
    Ezekiel Staggs: SW¼ NE¼ Sec. 26, 40.08 acres, cash, paid $50.10, patent 20 Sep 1839, certificate 20995.The forty directly below Thomas Graham's. Rebecca Staggs had married William sixteen years earlier.Tract book entryDocumented
  4. 8 Oct 1836
    James Goins: NW¼ NE¼ Sec. 33, 40.68 acres, cash, paid $50.86, patent 20 Sep 1839, certificate 20829.Sold this forty to Thomas Graham on 13 May 1838, between entry and patent. That is why no Graham name appears on it.Tract book entryDocumented
  5. 6 May 1844
    Thomas Graham, Jr: NE¼ SE¼ Sec. 29, 40.02 acres, cash, paid $50.03, patent 10 Jul 1844, certificate 23835. Written "Thomas Grimes, Jr".Entry to patent in nine weeks, the fastest in the family.Tract book entryDocumented
  6. 23 Nov 1852
    William Graham: S½ NW¼ (part) Sec. 27, 40.10 acres, cash, paid $50.13, certificate 27296. Written "William Grimes".Tract book entryDocumented
  7. 12 Mar 1853
    William Graham: NE¼ SE¼ Sec. 28, 40.13 acres, cash, paid $50.17, certificate 27637. Written "William Grimes".Adjoining his father's 1836 forty.Tract book entryDocumented
  8. 21 Dec 1854
    Thomas Graham: SW¼ SE¼ and SW¼ NE¼ Sec. 28 and 33, 80.82 acres, graduated, paid $10.11, certificate 31399.Eighty acres for ten dollars. The Graduation Act had just cut the price by ninety percent.Tract book entryDocumented
  9. 22 Dec 1854
    William Graham: SE¼ SE¼ Sec. 29, 40.03 acres, graduated, paid $5.01, patent 1858, certificate 31397. Written "William Grimes".One day after Thomas. They went to Tuscaloosa together.Tract book entryDocumented
  10. 11 Dec 1858
    Thomas Graham: W½ SE¼ Sec. 29, 80.05 acres, graduated, paid $10.01, patent 2 Jul 1860, certificate 37133.The last Graham purchase before the war.Tract book entryDocumented
