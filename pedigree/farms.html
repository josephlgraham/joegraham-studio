Farm records, 1860 to 1880

What each family had on hand before and after the war, as the farm censuses of 1860, 1870, and 1880 wrote it down, and what Wilson's cavalry took in March 1865, as the Southern Claims files list it. Each record is shown in full on its era page; this page puts them all in one table to search and sort.

The 1860 and 1870 crops are for the year before the census (1859 and 1869); livestock were counted on 1 June. The label on each record is for who the farmer is; the figures are read off the page.

  • No 1850 farm census for Jefferson County has been found (Alabama Archives, Montgomery).
  • No Staggs farm appears on the farm census pages we have.
  • Only the family's own lines and the two Belcher lines are here. The other lines on these pages can be added page by page.
