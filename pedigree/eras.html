Eras

The family's records, era by era: who was alive, what the records say, the land, the farms, and the documents. The eras are the same ones the land map uses. Era names and their boundary years are general history.

  1. Native groundbefore 1814

    Before the Creek cession. The area that became Jefferson County was part of a large neutral ground between the Creek, Cherokee, Chickasaw, and Choctaw nations.

    6 people, 1 family records
  2. Territorial period1814 to 1819

    The Treaty of Fort Jackson (9 Aug 1814) took the land from the Creeks. It became part of Monroe County, Mississippi Territory, then Alabama Territory (1817). Jefferson County was created 13 Dec 1819; Alabama became a state the next day.

    6 people
  3. Early statehood1820 to 1835

    The government survey of T15S R3W (notes closed 22 Jun 1821) and the first patents. William and Rebecca married 30 Jan 1820.

    16 people, 14 family records, 7 documents
  4. Antebellum years1836 to 1860

    Most Graham land in the township was entered in these years (1836 to 1858).

    26 people, 38 family records, 10 land entries, 4 farm records, 20 documents
  5. Civil War1861 to 1865

    Thomas Graham was conscripted and died in January 1864 (Nancy's Southern Claims Commission file). Wilson's cavalry camped near William Graham's in March 1865 (Thomas James's claim).

    26 people, 24 family records, 4 farm records
  6. Reconstruction1865 to 1877

    The first family homestead claims (1870, 1871, 1877). Birmingham was founded in 1871.

    27 people, 18 family records, 3 land entries, 6 farm records, 5 documents
  7. New South1877 to 1900

    Iron, coal, and railroads around Birmingham. The last Graham-name patents in the township (1885 to 1891).

    37 people, 26 family records, 4 land entries, 2 farm records, 22 documents
  8. Progressive Era1900 to 1920

    The USGS surveyed the Birmingham Coal District sheet about 1904 to 1906; it names the home place Grimestown.

    36 people, 35 family records, 9 documents
  9. Between the wars and the Great Depression1920 to 1941

    The family book's hand-drawn Mount Olive map remembers the homeplaces of the early 1930s and 40s.

    41 people, 27 family records, 5 documents
  10. World War II1941 to 1945

    Lee Graham served with the 487th Bomb Group, Eighth Air Force.

    30 people, 18 family records, 11 documents
  11. Postwar and civil rights years1945 to 1975

    28 people, 44 family records, 40 documents
  12. Late twentieth century1975 to 2000

    17 people, 12 family records, 19 documents
  13. Today2000 to now

    The lidar under this map was flown 5 Feb to 27 Mar 2024.

    9 people, 9 family records, 2 documents
