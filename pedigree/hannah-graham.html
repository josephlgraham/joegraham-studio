Period context, not about this person

General history of the time, from published sources. Nothing here says this person had or did it.

  • The 1850 census was the first to try to collect information about every member of every household. Earlier censuses named the head and counted the rest by age and sex. Wikipedia, 1850 United States censuscontext
  • Rural cooking was mostly done at the hearth in iron: a pot, a lidded bake kettle (now called a Dutch oven) and a three-legged skillet called a spider. Cast-iron cookstoves were sold from the 1830s. The Henry Ford, Dutch oven bake kettle; Campbell Co. TNGenWeb, Dutch ovens and cookstovescontext
  • Hog killing came with the first hard cold, late November into January: lard rendered in iron wash pots, hams and sides salted for weeks, then smoked. Exchange Place, Hog and Hominy; Hilliard, Hog Meat and Hoecakecontext
  • Cornbread was baked fresh; poor farm families often had no bread but cornbread. NCpedia, Breads (citing Hilliard and Taylor)context
