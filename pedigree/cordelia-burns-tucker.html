Open questions

  • Rickwood Caverns: published accounts do not mention the Burns family. The Encyclopedia of Alabama says a Boy Scout troop and its leader, Eddie Rickles, discovered the caves in the early 1950s; Rickles and Sonny Arwood combined their names for the name; it ran privately from 1954 and opened as a state park in June 1974. It also notes graffiti from the 1890s. Eddie Rickles is a different person from Country Boy Eddie Burns. Encyclopedia of Alabama showcaves.com Encyclopedia of Alabama and showcaves.com
