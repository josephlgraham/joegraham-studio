The camera

Fujifilm X-T30 II

A small body with real dials on top: shutter speed, exposure comp, and a drive dial. I can change settings without digging through a menu.

At a glance
Sensor26.1 MP APS-C X-Trans CMOS 4
ProcessorX-Processor 4
Viewfinder0.39 in OLED electronic viewfinder
Screen3 in tilting touchscreen
ShutterMechanical to 1/4000 s, electronic to 1/32000 s
Video4K up to 30p
Weather sealingNone. More on that under the bags.
How mine is dressed

It never goes out plain. There’s a wood hand grip on the bottom, a square metal hood on the lens, a soft silicone eyecup on the viewfinder, and a patterned strap. I do like to accessorize. The full list is on the accessories page.

A circular polarizer lives on the front of whatever lens is mounted. It’s how I like to shoot, and it protects the lens glass in case anything happens. Why that works.
